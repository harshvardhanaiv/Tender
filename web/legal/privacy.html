Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

TenderFlow respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it under UK GDPR.

Data we collect

  • Account data: email, display name, Firebase user ID (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Usage data: searches, saved tenders, company profiles, credit ledger, AI analyses you run.
  • Payment data: processed by Stripe; we store customer/subscription IDs, not full card numbers.
  • Technical data: session cookies, IP address (for security and rate limiting).

How we use data

To provide the service, meter credits, process payments, send transactional email, improve reliability, and comply with law.

Processors

We use Firebase (Google), Stripe, DeepSeek (AI API), and our hosting provider. Data may be processed in the UK, EU, or US depending on provider.

Retention & deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting support; we will delete personal data within 30 days except where retention is required for billing or legal obligations.

Your rights

You have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your data, and to object to processing. Contact support@tenderflow.app or lodge a complaint with the ICO.

Cookies

We use essential session cookies for authentication. No advertising cookies.
